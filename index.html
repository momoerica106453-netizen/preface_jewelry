1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to PREFACE Pin Studio, an internal content planning, publishing and analytics tool operated solely for the PREFACE Jewelry Pinterest business account.

PREFACE Pin Studio is not offered as a public service and does not provide Pinterest account access or Pinterest data to customers, advertisers or other third parties.

PREFACE Pin Studio privacy summary

  • Operator: PREFACE Jewelry.
  • Purpose: internal Pinterest content planning, owner-approved Pin publishing, publishing-status tracking and performance analytics.
  • Account access: limited to the authorized PREFACE Jewelry Pinterest business account.
  • Human review: every Pin is reviewed and approved by the account owner before publication.
  • Third-party access: Pinterest account access and Pinterest data are not provided to third parties.
  • Data sale: Pinterest data is not sold or rented.

2. Purpose of the application

PREFACE Pin Studio is used to:

  • read the authorized account's Pinterest boards and related Pin information;
  • prepare Pinterest content using PREFACE Jewelry product information and brand-owned images;
  • publish Pins only after review and approval by the account owner;
  • record publishing status and operational results; and
  • retrieve Pin and account analytics to evaluate content performance.
All Pins are reviewed and approved by the PREFACE Jewelry account owner before publication.

3. Information we process

Depending on the permissions granted through Pinterest, the application may process:

  • Pinterest account identifiers and basic account metadata;
  • board names, board identifiers and board metadata;
  • Pin content, Pin identifiers, destination links and publishing status;
  • Pin and account analytics made available through the Pinterest API;
  • OAuth access tokens and authorization data needed to connect the authorized account;
  • PREFACE Jewelry product descriptions, links and brand-owned images; and
  • limited technical logs needed to diagnose errors and maintain application security.

The application does not request Pinterest passwords or session cookies. It accesses Pinterest information only through authorized Pinterest APIs and permissions granted by the account owner.

4. How we use information

Information is used only to operate PREFACE Pin Studio for PREFACE Jewelry, including content preparation, owner-approved publication, publishing-status tracking, performance reporting, troubleshooting and security.

We do not use Pinterest data for unrelated advertising profiles, data brokerage, credit decisions or automated decisions about individuals.

5. Sharing and disclosure

PREFACE Jewelry does not sell, rent or provide Pinterest account information or Pinterest API data to third parties. Data is not combined with data from unrelated Pinterest accounts.

Information may be disclosed only when required by law, necessary to protect the security and integrity of the application, or processed by infrastructure providers acting on our instructions and subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.

6. Data retention

Pinterest information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the internal tool, maintain publishing records, evaluate PREFACE Jewelry content performance, resolve technical issues and meet legal obligations.

Where Pinterest's developer rules require information to be refreshed rather than permanently stored, the application retrieves current information through the Pinterest API. Authorization data is removed or invalidated when access is revoked or the application is disconnected, subject to any limited retention required for security, legal or audit purposes.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss. Access credentials are kept confidential, access is limited to the account owner and authorized operators, and application permissions are limited to functions required for the stated purpose.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Access, revocation and deletion

The Pinterest account owner may revoke the application's access through Pinterest account settings or by contacting PREFACE Jewelry. Following a valid request, we will delete or de-identify information associated with the application unless retention is required by law or necessary for security and fraud prevention.

9. Pinterest and other services

Pinterest's handling of information is governed by its own Privacy Policy and terms. This policy describes PREFACE Jewelry's handling of information through PREFACE Pin Studio and does not replace Pinterest's policies.

This policy page is hosted using GitHub Pages. We do not intentionally use advertising trackers or analytics on this page. GitHub may process technical request data according to the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. Children's privacy

PREFACE Pin Studio is an internal business tool and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the application, Pinterest requirements or applicable law. The current version and its effective date will be posted on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, access revocation or deletion requests, contact PREFACE Jewelry through the official Instagram account @preface_jewelry.
